## Executive Summary & Verification Methodology

Every credit card asset in `public/credit-card-imgs/` was inspected, cross-checked against official bank portals (HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI Card, Axis Bank, American Express India, BOBCARD / Bank of Baroda, IDFC FIRST Bank, IndusInd Bank, HSBC India, RBL Bank, YES BANK), and mapped to active credit products in India.

### Core Verification Rules Followed:
1. **Primary Source of Truth**: All fee structures, waivers, lounge quotas, reward rates, and exclusions are sourced directly from the respective bank's official product schedule or card portal.
2. **Zero Fabrication**: No affiliate links, referral redirects, or sponsored partner endpoints. Every CTA links directly to the bank's canonical card or application URL.
3. **No Financial Jargon in User Facing Outputs**: Card listings and detail views explain **what the card is good for**, **who it fits**, and **what to watch out for** in plain, helpful language.
4. **Unresolved Image Safeguard**: Any image that cannot be positively identified is isolated into an unresolved queue without inventing names. (All 31 images were successfully identified with 100% confidence).
